## Winterland 0.9.0 "Ages" ❄️

The world grows up. Survival is now **era 1** of a ten-era climb, and every era brings its own look, machines and main resource.

### What's new

- **The climb to era 10.** Each new era opens once every upgrade from the earlier eras reaches the next multiple of 10 and your settlement reaches the next multiple of 5 (era 2 needs level 10 everywhere and settlement 5). Upgrades now go up to level 100, the settlement to 50, and each era costs ten times the last, so later eras feel properly earned.
- **Hands-on pads, made lovelier.** Upgrading is still all about walking to a pad and standing on it. The pads got softer glows, gentler sounds and more satisfying feedback. There's no auto-buying, by design.
- **Cosy era palettes.** Every era follows the new art guide's warm colours, and the era 2 and 10 ground is slightly darker so it reads well against the snow.
- **Comfort settings.** A new Comfort tab has Reduce motion, Full / Calm / Minimal effects, and a colour-blind friendly palette.
- **Smoother late game.** Repeated items, people and carts are drawn together, which cuts late-game draw calls by about 40%. The busiest scene now peaks at 412 (0.8.0 peaked at 967).
- **Calmer crowds.** Townsfolk get stuck and jammed much less often.
- **Era 2 to 10 content (preview).** The models for 32 new machines and a hand-gathered main resource in every era are in. They get wired into play in 0.10 to 0.12.

### Your save

Saves from 0.8.0 and earlier load as they are. They stay in the same place:
`~/.config/Winterland` (Linux), `%APPDATA%\Winterland` (Windows), `~/Library/Application Support/Winterland` (macOS).
